FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play Hoops Trivia?

Look at the player's photo, then pick the correct name from three multiple-choice options. On harder levels the face is pixelated, so you'll need to rely on clues like the jersey, build, and team colors.

What is the difference between Hard and Super Hard modes?

In Hard Mode faces are partially pixelated. In Super Hard (Legend Mode) the pixelation is heavy — only players with deep NBA knowledge will recognize the stars from the blur.

How is my score calculated?

You earn points for each correct answer. The game tracks both your current score and your all-time personal record, so you can always try to beat your best.

Is Hoops Trivia affiliated with the NBA?

No. This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NBA or any of its teams.
