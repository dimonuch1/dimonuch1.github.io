FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions

Does TV Vibe stream movies or TV shows?

No. TV Vibe does not stream any content — it's a tracker that helps you organize what you watch and find where it's available to stream.

Where does the show and movie data come from?

TV Vibe is powered by TMDB, giving you accurate, frequently updated ratings, cast info, and streaming availability.

Can I track individual episodes?

Yes. You can mark single episodes or entire seasons as watched, and your progress is tracked automatically across every show.

Do I need an account to use TV Vibe?

An account keeps your watchlist and progress backed up and synced. You can sign in with Apple, Google, or an email and password.
