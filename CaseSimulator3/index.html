FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an official CS:GO app?

No, Case Simulator 3 is a fan-made simulator app and is not affiliated with Valve or CS:GO. It is for entertainment purposes only.

Can I win real items or money?

No. All skins and items in the app are virtual and have no real-world value. The app is a simulator for fun only.

How does the Jackpot mode work?

In Jackpot mode you wager your virtual skins against other players for a chance to win their collection. It's all in-app and no real money is involved.

How do I appear on the Leaderboard?

Keep opening cases and collecting rare skins. The more valuable your inventory, the higher you rank on the global leaderboard.
